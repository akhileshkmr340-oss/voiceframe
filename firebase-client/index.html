Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every model is open source and runs on free services and a free GPU. There's no watermark and no paid plan. Because the GPU is shared, there's a daily limit on films with AI motion per person.

How long does a film take?

The story, pictures and voices are ready in about 10 minutes. AI motion and lip-sync take about 1½ hours for a 5-minute film on the free GPU. Keep the tab open; you can watch shots arrive as they're made.

Can I monetise the videos?

The story, pictures, voices and music are generated for your film, and a licence report lists every source. Platforms ask you to label AI content, and the upload description includes that label. VoiceFrame can't guarantee copyright clearance, so review your film before publishing.

Which languages are supported?

126 languages for the story, the voices and the captions: English, 18 Indian languages (Hindi, Bhojpuri, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Kannada, Gujarati, Maithili, Magahi, Chhattisgarhi, Tamil, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, Urdu, Konkani and Sindhi), and world languages from Arabic, Spanish and French to Swahili and Japanese. Right-to-left scripts such as Arabic and Urdu get right-to-left captions. A few languages have no native free voice yet and are read with a close language's pronunciation.

Can I fix a shot I don't like?

Yes. In the storyboard, pick the shots and repaint, re-animate or re-sync just those. You can also edit a line, reorder shots or use your own photo or clip.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in Chrome or Edge on a computer or phone. You can also install it as an app from the browser menu.

Where are my films stored?

On your own device, in the browser. Download the MP4 to keep a copy elsewhere.
