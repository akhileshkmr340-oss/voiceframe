From one sentence to a finished film

1

Speak or write

Dictate your idea in English or Hindi, or type it. Voice commands like “create film” work too.

2

Full story

An AI writer expands it into a complete story with a beginning, a turn and an ending, split into cinematic scenes.

3

Character design

Each character gets a look, a personality, a portrait and a voice, and stays consistent across scenes.

4

Scenes & camera

Every scene is painted and given a camera move: push-ins, pans, light, grain and colour grading.

5

Voice & score

A neural narrator voices the story. An original soundtrack is composed for your film alone.

6

Download & post

Export MP4 in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, then share to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn or WhatsApp.

Review rights before publishing

Each video includes a source report. Generated content and source licences still need review; this app cannot guarantee copyright clearance.

  • Original story. Prompts are screened for franchise characters, brands and song lyrics. Those get turned into original creations.
  • Generated visuals. Scenes are AI-generated for your film, or public-domain (CC0/PDM) stock with full source records.
  • Unique music. The soundtrack is synthesised note by note in your browser, so there's no third-party track to trigger Content ID.
  • Synthetic voice. Narration comes from the open Kokoro-82M voice model (Apache-2.0). No real person's voice is cloned.
  • AI disclosure. Your upload description includes the AI-content label YouTube and Meta ask for.
